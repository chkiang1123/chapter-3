    Solve 28 rounds following Teaching Examples 4.1 to 4.10, in order. Each round picks one of 4 versions of the question at random. Choose a balanced move, apply it to both sides, then write the new equation.

    How to play

    • Choose a move, then apply it to both sides of the equation.
    • Type the new line after simplifying. Answers can be integers, fractions such as −20/3, or decimals.
    • Use the highlight button to tell variable terms from constants. Use a hint if you get stuck.
    • You may enter a line up to 2 steps ahead. The skipped steps still count.
    • If you make a mistake in any step, you must also solve one similar question straight after it.
    • 10 points for each step solved without a hint or retry; 5 points after a hint or retry. Similar questions give no points.
    • Your progress is saved after every question, so you can stop and continue later.
